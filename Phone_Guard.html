Phone Guard

Personal Android security checklist for your phone.

Quick check

  • ✓ Keep Android/HyperOS and apps updated.
  • ✓ Google Play Protect: ON.
  • ✓ Screen lock: PIN/password + biometrics.
  • ✓ Review Accessibility and Device Admin apps.
  • ✓ Review apps with SMS, microphone, camera, contacts and location access.
  • ✓ Remove apps you do not recognize.
  • ✓ Turn on Find My Device.
  • ✓ Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, recovery codes or API secret keys.

Important

This is a security assistant/checklist, not a background antivirus. A webpage cannot continuously monitor Android apps, files, SMS or network traffic. A real background agent requires an Android app with explicit permissions.
